# Changelog

Each version applies to a clean Japanese ROM: the Anime Movie Edition (CRC32 82C1282F) or, from v0.9.2, the CG Movie Edition (CRC32 6BEA837E), using the matching patch. Always patch a clean ROM; don't apply a new version on top of an old one.

## v0.9.2 – Synopsis and CG Movie Edition

Patched ROM CRC32: **65DA96E9** (Anime Movie Edition), **B01B8034** (CG Movie Edition)

### Added
- **CG Movie Edition patch.** A separate patch for *Tales of Hearts – CG Movie Edition (Japan)*. The two editions share all game data except the movies, so the translation is identical.
- **Synopsis menu translated.** All 48 entries, with their chapter titles, are now in English. They are written as the party's shared diary, as in the Japanese: Kor writes most pages, and Hisui, Beryl, Kohaku, Ines, Kunzite, Lithia and Chalcedony each add their own in their own voice.
- Names and terms match the rest of the translation (Spiria Core, Despir, Gardenia, Wanderlust Inc., Lion Park Grand Prix, and so on), and the coloured highlights on key names and places are kept.

### Please check
- Synopsis text is sized to the Japanese text, not measured on the real screen yet. Report any page where text runs off the right edge or the bottom.

## v0.9 – First public test build

Patched ROM CRC32: F86AF16E

### Added
- All story scenes, skits (including choices and hint skits), NPC dialogue and side quests, based on the official *Tales of Hearts R* English script.
- Menus, battle interface, tutorials, shops and save/load.
- Items, materials, accessories, Somas, artes, skills, titles, enemies and the Monster Book.
- Town-name banners and title-screen buttons.
- Labels drawn as pictures in the menus: shop columns, camp menu, Curestone settings, status screens, battle-mode tabs, formation, town & dungeon list, name-entry keyboard and slot machine.
